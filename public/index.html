A change in outcome on a user-supplied commit is evidence of inconsistency, not proof of a flaky test or its cause. Environment and test data can also differ.

THE WORKFLOW

to a useful conversation.

BUILT FOR THE WORK AFTER CODE

Less guessing. Better questions.

Flakewise helps developers investigate inconsistent test outcomes using reports they already have. It does not execute tests, connect to your repository, or automatically fix or quarantine them.
